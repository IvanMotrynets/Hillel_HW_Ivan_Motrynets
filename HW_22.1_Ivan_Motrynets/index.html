Tourist Transcarpathia: Mountains, Castles and Lakes

Transcarpathia combines highland scenery, authentic villages, unique lakes and historic sites — an ideal region for travel year‑round.

1. Synevyr — the "Sea" of the Carpathians

Short: Synevyr National Nature Reserve is one of the largest and most famous high‑mountain lakes in Ukraine, often called the “sea” in the mountains. What to do: walk around the lake, visit the observation tower, see the nearby brown bear rehabilitation center. Why visit: spectacular views, fresh air, and the chance to combine ecotourism with cultural routes.

https://synevyr-park.in.ua/

2. Mukachevo and Palanok Castle

Short: Palanok Castle in Mukachevo is a multi‑century fortification that rises above the town. What to do: take a guided tour of the castle, view exhibits, enjoy city panoramas from the bastions. Why visit: a mix of history and architecture with easy access from Transcarpathian transport routes.

https://karpatium.com.ua/pamyatky-arkhitektury/zamok-palanok

3. Kosino and thermal resorts (Velyatyno, Kosino)

Short: Transcarpathia is known for health and thermal resorts — thermal complexes in Kosyno and other relaxation spots. What to do: enjoy thermal pools, spa treatments, and short excursions around the resorts. Why visit: combine comfortable relaxation with natural and cultural sightseeing.

https://kosino.ua/

Plan routes by season: summer for mountain hikes and lakes; autumn and winter for castles, thermal baths and gastronomy.
